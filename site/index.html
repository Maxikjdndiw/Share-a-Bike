Safe by default.

Clear rules for riders, peace of mind for parents and owners.

  • Helmet required

    No helmet, no ride. On every trip.

  • Checked every week

    Brakes, lights and tyres, every single week.

    No tracking when a bike is parked.

    Location data is removed automatically.

    The app warns you before icy rides.

    No rides while school is out.

Good questions.

Anything else? Write to us at hello@example.com.

When does it launch?

The pilot starts in January 2027 at Schule Louisenlund. Everyone on the waitlist hears first.

What if a bike is broken?

Report it in the app. We set the bike to maintenance right away and repair it before anyone rides it again.

Can I lend my bike only on certain days?

Yes. You set the days and times your bike is available, and you can change them at any time.

How do I pay?

By card or bank transfer through a payment provider. We never store your card details ourselves.

Is my data safe?

Location is only recorded during rides and deleted after 30 days. You only ever see your own data.

What if I return a bike late?

You get a short block from the service, so the next rider is not left waiting.
