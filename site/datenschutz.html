Datenschutz­erklärung

In short (English)

  • We only collect what you type into the waitlist form: name, school email, class or year, whether you want to ride or lend, your bike type (owners only) and your two confirmations.
  • We use your data only to run the waitlist and to tell you about the launch. We never sell it or use it for advertising.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no Google services, no external fonts. Only your choice in the cookie notice is kept in your browser's local storage (see Cookies).
  • Unconfirmed sign ups are deleted after 7 days. All waitlist data is deleted at the latest 3 months after the launch, or any time you ask us.
  • Write to mail.shareabike@gmail.com to see, change or delete your data.
