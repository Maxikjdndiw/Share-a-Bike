Cookies

In short

  • This website sets no cookies. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no social media plugins, no Google services.
  • When you close the cookie notice, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage (key shareABike.cookieChoice, value "accept" or "decline"), so the notice doesn't show again. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • "Accept" and "Decline" do the same thing here, because there is nothing optional to switch on.
  • When you join the waitlist, the form data is sent to our database (see the privacy notice). That doesn't use cookies either.

Remove the stored choice

Clear the site data for this website in your browser settings. The notice will show again on your next visit.

Kurz auf Deutsch

Diese Website setzt keine Cookies und verwendet keine Analyse- oder Werbedienste. Nur Ihre Auswahl im Cookie-Hinweis wird lokal in Ihrem Browser gespeichert (technisch notwendig, § 25 Abs. 2 TDDDG), damit der Hinweis nicht erneut erscheint. Diese Information verlässt Ihr Gerät nicht.
