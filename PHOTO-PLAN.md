# Photo plan: Share a Bike waitlist site

Shot list for the team, in priority order. All photos are **our own** (no stock photos). They can be
taken with a recent phone. The app mockups and the bike drawings stay; photos add the one thing they
can't: proof that this is a real project at a real school.

## Before anyone takes a picture

- **School permission** for photos on the grounds and for showing buildings. Ask before shooting.
- **Consent for every recognisable person.** Under 18: written consent from a parent as well
  (German law: right to one's own image, KUG, plus GDPR). Keep the signed forms.
- **No other students in the background** without consent. Shoot at quiet times or crop them out.
- **No school logo or crest** on the site without the school's written OK.

## Style rules, so the photos look like one set

- Natural colour, no filters, no heavy contrast. Daylight, preferably the same season.
- Level horizon, nothing important at the edges, leave space where text or phones go.
- Corners rounded like the cards (24 px; 32 px for big pictures), so they match the page.
- No mint-coloured props; the accent stays reserved for buttons and highlights.

## Hand-off to build

- Send originals (full size, JPEG/HEIC). They'll be converted to AVIF/WebP at 1x/2x, kept under
  about 300 KB each, lazy-loaded below the first screen, with alt text describing what's in the
  picture (e.g. "Bike racks next to the main building at Schule Louisenlund").
- Each picture gets a short caption only where it helps (place and what it shows).
- Photos from open archives (e.g. Wikimedia Commons) are a fallback for #3 and #6 only. They need a
  credit line in the footer, and they can't be searched from the current build environment.
